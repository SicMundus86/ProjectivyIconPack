Step #1:  Enable Developer Options on your TV device (i.e. Google TV / Android TV)
	-  [Google TV Device]:  
			a. Android Settings >> System >> About
			b. Scroll down to "Build" and keep pressing it until you see a message that "you are now a developer"
	-  [Android TV Device]:  
			a. Android Settings >> Device Preferences >> About
			b. Scroll down to "Build" and keep pressing it until you see a message that "you are now a developer"
		
		
Step #2:  Enable ADB Capability
	-  [Google TV Device]:  
			a. Android Settings >> System >> Developer Options
			b. "USB Debugging" --> ENABLE
	-  [Android TV Device]:  
			a. Android Settings >> Device Preferences >> Developer Options
			b. "ADB Debugging" --> ENABLE
			c. "Network debugging" --> ENABLE


Step #3:  Grab the application package name from the installed app from your TV device
	a.  From Projectivy Launcher homescreen, click on the washer icon at the top right corner
	b.  Click on All apps
	c.  Locate the app you want to have the icon mapped
	d.  Long-press the app and click on App settings (aka android app settings)
	e.  Notate the application package name right below version number of the App Name


Step #4:  Locate your Local Network IP Address (Streaming device and phone must be connected to same network)
	-  [Google TV Device]:  
			a. Android Settings >> System >> About >> Status
			b. IP Address (typically starts with "192.168...."
	-  [Android TV Device]:
			a. Android Settings >> Device Preferences >> About >> Status
			b. IP Address (typically starts with "192.168...."


Step #5:  Install App Called ATVTools on Either Your iPhone or Android phone
	-  [Android Phone]:  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.vodik7.atvtools
	-  [Apple iPhone]:  https://apps.apple.com/app/id1661419573


Step #6:  Pair your TV Device to ATVTools from your phone
	a.  Start the app ATVTools from your phone
	b.  ATVTools should automatically start scanning for your TV devices
	c.  Look for the device with the IP address matching from Step #4 and click on that device
	d.  Follow the instructions on both your phone and TV device screens to "ALLOW" network debugging
			-  Select "Always"
			-  Select "Allow"
	e.  You should now be connect to ATVTools between your phone and TV device.  You can confirm this by seeing it say "Connected" at the top.


Step #7:   On ATVTools from your phone, use an ADB command to grab the full activity verbage 
	a.  Click on either the "Command" button (from iphone) or "Shell" button (from Android phone) from the bottom menu
	b.  Copy/paste command from Step C below
	c.  cmd package resolve-activity -c android.intent.category.LEANBACK_LAUNCHER ar.tvplayer.tv
	d.  Replace "ar.tvplayer.tv" with the actual application package name from Step #3 in the Shell Command text box
	e.  Click on the "Copy" button above the Shell Command text box to copy all the resulting text
	f.  Paste that long text into your github icon mapping report request
